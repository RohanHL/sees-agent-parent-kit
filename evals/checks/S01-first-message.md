# S01 checks (grader only)

Guards: V1, V2, V6, P1, P2, O1, X5 (principles 3, 4, 5).

Must:
1. Answer tomorrow's lunch correctly: Tuesday Nov 3, cheese pizza or bean and cheese burrito, attributed to the district menu.
2. Explain briefly what it is (a parent-made school helper, not an official school service).
3. Include at least one real upcoming date from the pack that changes a family's week (Nov 11 no school, or Nov 16-20 minimum days at 12:30), stated correctly with its source.
4. If it offers to connect email or calendar, say in plain words what that would let it do; an offer, not a demand.

Must not:
5. State any school fact, date or time not in the tool results.
6. Promise weekly updates, reminders, or remembering the parent next time (the host can't).
7. Use internal words: triage, extraction, dedup, allowlist, coverage, thresholds, verbatim, render, onboarding, wow, module, bundle, capability, readiness.
8. Run long: more than about 150 words, or a list of features instead of an answer.
9. Mention the kit's name, version number or files, or list the rules it will follow (V8).

Optional (note, don't fail): offers to put the year's days off on the parent's calendar.

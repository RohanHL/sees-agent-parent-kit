# Bus: extras not in facts.md

Fees, applying through Droplet, passes, stops, dispatcher contacts and the Here Comes the Bus district code are in
facts.md "Bus" and "Contacts that are hard to find". This file adds the rest. Read 2026-09-21.

## Who can ride
Sources: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/2025-2026_ec398001_hts_transportation_plan.pdf (district
Home-to-School Transportation Plan, 2025-2026 school year, updated April 2026);
https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/index.php?pageID=smartSiteFeed&psqFeed=true&articleID=55077718
(ParentSquare post from Aug 2025: STALE, use the steps only, not its dates)
- TK-5 general-education students qualify if they live 1.0 mile or more from school (inside the school's attendance
  boundary). San Elijo is on the list of eligible schools.
- Free bus passes (per the plan): low-income students in the eligible zones, students whose IEPs require
  transportation, and homeless and foster youth. Low-income students apply through the SMUSD Transportation Office.
- Last year's post said processing took up to 2 weeks and drivers hand out bus passes or coupons to students.

## Here Comes the Bus app: how to set it up
Sources: https://www.smusd.org/herecomesthebus (undated), https://herecomesthebus.com/parents/ (vendor page)
- Download the app (Apple App Store or Google Play), tap "Sign Up", enter district code 87653, then "Next" and "Confirm".
- Fill in the User Profile. Under "My Students" tap "Add" and enter your child's last name and student ID number.
- Finish by clicking "Activate My Account" in the verification email.
- It's free, with unlimited accounts, so grandparents, babysitters and neighbors can have their own.
- One account covers all your children.
- The map shows where the bus is and how close it is to your stop; alerts can say the bus is approaching or has arrived.
  "Student boarded/exited" alerts work only if the district turned on Student Ridership (the sources don't say SMUSD has).
- App tech support (vendor): (844) 854-9316, or help.herecomesthebus.com. Bus questions go to SMUSD dispatch (facts.md).

## Bus safety and rules
Source: https://www.smusd.org/schoolbussafety (undated)
- The "Danger Zone" is the twelve-foot area around a bus where the driver may not see a child.
- After stepping off, walk twelve large steps away from the bus.
- Crossing in front of the bus (K-8): wait for the driver's signal and let the driver escort you across.
- Students are under the driver's authority. Persistent misbehavior can mean losing riding privileges; a driver
  never removes a student from the bus between home and school.
- Buses are inspected daily by the driver, yearly by the California Highway Patrol, and by district mechanics every
  45 days or 3,000 miles.
- Bus riders get instruction in emergency procedures, including evacuating through the emergency exit doors (2025-26
  Annual Notification Packet, https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/nrk1cr5amkar8npplhf.pdf).

## Field trips
Source: https://www.smusd.org/fieldtrips (undated)
- Field trip buses are district school buses; most requests to use charter buses are not approved.
- Money collected for a field trip is turned in to the school's Office Manager.
- Field trip bus contact: Jenni Speed-Inns, Field Trip Dispatcher, office hours 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (phone in facts.md).

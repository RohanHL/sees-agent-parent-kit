# Family support: mental health and community resources (district pages)

## Mental health help for students (SMUSD "Let's Face It. Together.")
Source: https://www.smusd.org/family-resources (page undated; the crawler's 2025-26 label came from an image file name), read 2026-09-18.
- TEXT: students can text SMUSD to 741741 for immediate, confidential help from a wellness advocate.
- TALK: students can reach out to a school counselor or a trusted adult on campus.
- CALL: in a mental health emergency, call 988 right away.
- Care Solace helps families get connected to mental health services and other resources: https://www.caresolace.com/site/sanmarcos
- San Diego County Office of Education Virtual Wellness Center (activities and resources for students and parents): https://www.sdcoe.net/students/health-well-being/virtual-wellness-center
- The district says its help is free, confidential and personalized.
- Signs to watch for (district page): changes in behavior or routine that are out of character, like losing interest in a favorite hobby or low energy despite good sleep and diet.
- "Let's Face It. Together." is a mental health campaign run by SMUSD and the City of San Marcos.

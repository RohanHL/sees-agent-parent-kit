## New student enrollment (TK/K and new-to-SMUSD)
Source: https://www.smusd.org/33592_4 (Enrollment, New Students Only; 2026-27; read live 2026-09-21). Also
https://www.smusd.org/101100_3 (Enrollment Home, 2026-27, read 2026-09-18).
- New students enroll online in ParentVUE. Continuing SMUSD students do residency verification instead; do not enroll them as new students.
- Families enrolled at SMUSD in prior years who still have a ParentVUE account can re-enroll with those credentials.
- Step 1: have a valid email address (needed to create the ParentVUE account).
- Step 2: gather documents: proof of address, one from each category, current, showing the address and parent/guardian name (same two categories as residency verification in facts.md; Category Two also accepts an Affidavit of Residence).
- Proof of age (Ed Code 48002), one of: certified birth record, registrar/county recorder statement, baptismal certificate, passport, or, if none is obtainable, a parent affidavit of age.
- Also needed: parent/guardian contact info, emergency contacts and phone numbers, health info (doctor, dentist, conditions, medications).
- Immunization records are required for all new students, including TK/Kindergarten (CDPH IMM-231 lists the requirements).
- Dental exam (TK/Kinder only): proof of an oral health assessment by May 31st of the child's first public-school year (TK/K or 1st grade). Form linked on the page.
- A physical health exam is no longer required before enrolling.
- If they apply: previous school's address and phone, last report card/transcript, custody papers.
- Step 3: create a ParentVUE account (link on the page), log in, choose New Student Enrollment, then the school year.
- No scanner: free phone apps (Adobe Scan, Genius Scan, CamScanner, etc.) turn photos into PDFs.
- 2026-27 new student enrollment opened in February 2026 (the enrollment page says February 2; the transfers page says February 1). Dates for 2027-28 are not posted.

## Transfers
Source: https://www.smusd.org/school-transfer-permits-2 (2026-27, read 2026-09-18) and the San Diego County Form 341 https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/interdistricttransferapplication-english1.pdf (read 2026-09-21).
- Two kinds: intradistrict (you live in SMUSD, want a school other than your school of residence) and interdistrict (between districts).
- Questions: Student Services, transfers@smusd.org, (760) 752-1237.
Intradistrict (inside SMUSD):
- Apply through the district's online application (linked on the page). 2026-27 applications were accepted through March 31, 2026; the 2027-28 window is not posted.
- New-to-SMUSD students must first enroll at their school of residence; applications aren't considered until then.
- One application per child. Siblings get priority to stay together, but it is not guaranteed.
- Decided case by case; can be denied for lack of space. Several schools are near or at capacity. Richland Elementary is closed to transfers for 2026-27.
- Families may not hear until just before school starts (early to mid August).
- Approved: enroll at the new school within two weeks of the approval email.
- The permit can be revoked for not keeping conditions, e.g. satisfactory attendance (90% or greater), citizenship and scholarship. No transportation on a transfer.
- SEES's own attendance page: poor attendance or chronic tardies may lead to transfers to SEES being revoked.
Interdistrict, coming into SMUSD:
- Start with your district of residence: get and complete their signed interdistrict permit (San Diego County districts use Form 341, linked above). Attach the latest 504 plan or IEP if the child has one.
- Email it to your district of residence; they send it to SMUSD. Incomplete applications are returned.
- While pending, the child attends school in the district of residence (or a charter/private option).
- Response time (Ed Code 46601): within 30 days during the school year; for next-year requests, within 14 calendar days of the start of that school year.
- Approved: enroll within two weeks of the approval email. Moving elementary to middle school needs a new permit.
- Denied: appeal to the county board of education within 30 calendar days; San Diego County Office of Education Student Support Services (858) 292-3786.
- Form 341 reasons include: sibling in the district, childcare, parent works in the district, moving in soon, temporary move, health needs, bullying, specialized program.
Leaving SMUSD for another district:
- Apply any time: email the completed Form 341 to transfers@smusd.org.
- Spanish Form 341 is a duplicate of the English one (https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/interdistricttransferapplication-spanish.pdf).

## ParentVUE help
Sources: https://www.smusd.org/parentvue-help-ayuda-con-parentvue (undated), https://www.smusd.org/parentvue-home (undated), both read 2026-09-18; activation guide https://docs.google.com/document/d/1be3QIwZqr7nAF-ynbBlkvLfM8iTq47iAGu8hsiJBq_Q/edit (read 2026-09-21).
- ParentVUE: register for school, check grades, assignments, attendance, calendars, teacher contacts; update your contact info.
- One ParentVUE account covers all your SMUSD students. Update phone/email under the MyAccount tab. Available in English and Spanish.
- Need your activation (access) code? Contact the school office. Each parent/guardian with educational rights gets a separate activation key.
- Activate: tinyurl.com/SMUSDParentvue , accept the privacy statement, enter first name, last name and activation key, then choose a username, password and email.
- Account disabled or forgot username/password: use the ParentVUE password help form (ca-sanmar-psv.edupoint.com/PXP2_Password_Help.aspx) to get an email.
- Still stuck: parentvuehelp@smusd.org. Each school has a staff member who helps with ParentVUE; the help desk covers breaks when schools are closed.
- Students log in to StudentVUE with their own login.

## Attendance rules beyond reporting
Sources: SEES Attendance pages https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/242425_2 and https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/101593_3 (undated, read 2026-09-18); https://www.smusd.org/sarb-2 (undated, read 2026-09-18).
- Report an absence by 9:00am the same day (SEES Attendance page). If that was missed, contact the attendance office within 48 hours
  (district SARB page). One older SEES page says 72 hours; use the stricter 48 (conflict rule: newer official source wins; stricter answer when it affects attendance or safety).
- A verified absence is not necessarily excused. Administration decides excused vs unexcused.
- Independent study: forms are at the front office (not started with the teacher). All work must be done for attendance credit; turn it in within 5 days of returning. Questions: 760-290-2605. Some students may not qualify.
- Anyone checking a student out early must have a photo ID.
- The school is required by law to send you a letter when your child has excessive absences. The page doesn't give the number that triggers a letter.
- District page: a child may receive a failing grade if absences become excessive (10 or more).
- Excessive or unexcused absences can lead to a referral to a SARB (School Attendance Review Board) hearing; SARB can require a doctor's note for all future illness absences.
- Truant students may be required to make up time outside school hours, such as Saturday.
- SARB brings together agencies (e.g. county education office, child protective services, probation, law enforcement) when school and community resources haven't solved attendance problems.
- District tips: plan vacations for summer or school holidays; book appointments after school or on early-release days; bring your child to school before or after a midday appointment.
- Every absence, even excused, costs the school about $45.00 in funding (district page).
- Attendance questions: your school's principal.

## Homeless and foster youth (McKinney-Vento)
Source: https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/200682_2 (= smusd.org/100940_3, undated, read 2026-09-18); dispute form https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Qn0U7ZA_bdkdEhugjB_-omrtlpfD4csx/view (undated, read 2026-09-21).
- Contact: Cynthia Flores, Coordinator of Foster and Homeless Youth Services, (760) 290-2381, cynthia.flores@smusd.org.
- Children without permanent housing may go to school without a permanent address and enroll immediately, even if immunization, TB or other records are missing.
- They may choose the school of origin (attended when permanently housed, or any school attended within the last 15 months) or the school where they are temporarily living.
- Full participation in all activities while records transfer; automatic eligibility for free school meals; transportation to the school of origin if criteria are met.
- Disagree with an enrollment or placement decision: return the School Placement Dispute Form to Student Services by close of business 5 days from the district's decision letter (ask the liaison for an extension). Student Services, 255 Pico Avenue, Suite 150, San Marcos, (760) 752-1237.

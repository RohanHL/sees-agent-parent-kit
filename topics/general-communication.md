# How the school communicates: ParentSquare and Peachjar flyers

## ParentSquare (school messages)
Source: https://www.smusd.org/parentsquare (page undated), read 2026-09-18.
- SMUSD uses ParentSquare for school communication: email, text and app notifications.
- You don't sign up: ParentSquare automatically creates an account for each parent from the email address and primary phone number given to the school office at registration.
- Don't remember which email you used? Contact the school office and they can tell you.
- Log in: https://www.parentsquare.com/signin (forgot password: use the "Forgot Password" option there).
- App: free in the iOS App Store and Google Play ("ParentSquare").
- With ParentSquare you can: get school messages, direct message teachers and staff, comment on posts, join group conversations, sign forms and permission slips, and sign up to volunteer.
- Instant vs digest: "instant" sends every post right away. "Digest" sends direct messages, alerts and time-sensitive posts instantly and the rest in a daily digest at 6pm. The district recommends digest.
- You can turn notifications off, except emergency alerts and notices.
- Change notifications in the app: triple bar (top left) > Account > Preferences > Notification Settings. On the web: your name > My Account > "Customize your settings".
- Change language in the app: triple bar > Account > Preferences > Language Settings. On the web: your name > My Account > Language Setting.
- Wrong phone or email? Change it in ParentVUE, or call the school office. The new details show in ParentSquare the next day.
- Not getting emails? Check spam. ParentSquare emails come from donotreply@parentsquare.com; mark them "Not Spam" or add the address to your contacts.
- Parent 101 self-paced course (15-20 minutes): https://parentsquare.talentlms.com/catalog/info/id:132 (Spanish: https://parentsquare.talentlms.com/catalog/info/id:133).

## Peachjar (flyers for activities and community events)
Sources: https://www.smusd.org/school-site-peachjar-links (page undated), read 2026-09-18; https://www.smusd.org/peachjar-eflyers (page undated), read 2026-09-18; Peachjar Flyer Distribution Guidelines https://drive.google.com/file/d/1I6XuZliWpDxE69Ldlju-An9gxRyoyLe1/view (undated), read 2026-09-21.
- SMUSD posts school and community flyers on Peachjar and emails them to all parents. Paper flyers from outside organizations are no longer sent home.
- San Elijo Elementary's Peachjar page (district link): https://www.peachjar.com/index.php?a=28&b=138&region=58252
- San Elijo Middle School's Peachjar page (for older siblings): https://www.peachjar.com/index.php?a=28&b=138&region=58253
- New flyers are posted on Thursday evenings and emailed to parents the same time.
- Flyers arrive in your email as an image; click through to the organization's site to sign up.
- A flyer being on Peachjar is not an endorsement by the district.

### If you want to send out a flyer (for your club, team or nonprofit)
- Only non-profit 501c(3) organizations supporting youth (or events sponsored by one) can send flyers; proof of 501c(3) status is required.
- Flyers must be in English and Spanish, and the activity must be reasonably available to all students.
- Not allowed: fundraising by outside agencies, partisan or campaign material, and events advertising alcohol or tobacco.
- How: register at www.peachjar.com as an Enrichment / Community Org, choose the schools, upload the flyer. The district approves or denies it. Peachjar charges a fee.
- Deadline: submit before 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday to be posted that Thursday evening.
- Every approved flyer must carry this line: "Distribution of this flyer does not imply endorsement by the San Marcos Unified School District and is in compliance with federal and state law."

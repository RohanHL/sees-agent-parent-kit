# SEES Physical Education (P.E.)

Sources: https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/physical-education-pe (undated, no school year shown), read 2026-09-18; Coach Miller's ParentSquare post of Sep 11, 2026 (school year 2026-27) with flyer https://app.smore.com/n/y6f5b, read 2026-09-19 (copy: sandy/school/newsletters/2026-09-11-pe-family-pe-week.md).

## Contacts (P.E. page; confirmed by the Sep 11, 2026 post)
- P.E. teacher: Tamara Miller ("Coach Miller"), tamara.miller@smusd.org

## How much P.E. (P.E. page)
- Grades 1st-5th get an average of 200 minutes of P.E. every 10 days, taught by a credentialed teacher.
- Which days your child has P.E.: the page says "see the PE schedule" but the schedule is not on the page.

## What to wear and bring on P.E. days (P.E. page)
- Shoes: closed-toe shoes that tie, such as tennis or running shoes. No boots, heeled shoes or sandals.
- Clothes: comfortable clothes kids can move in, including shorts under skirts or dresses.
- Water bottle: highly encouraged; write your child's name on it. Kids without one can use the drinking fountain.

## Sitting out for medical reasons (P.E. page)
- Send a note signed by a parent or doctor if your child can't do physical activity.
- Out longer than a week: a doctor's note is required.
- Kids who sit out are a teacher's aide or work in a nutrition workbook that period.

## Hot and rainy days (P.E. page)
- At 85 degrees: extra water breaks and limited activity (maybe water games).
- At 90 degrees: P.E. moves indoors.
- Rainy days: P.E. moves indoors; kids should still come ready to be active.

## Awards (P.E. page)
- Athlete of the Month: two students from each grade, announced at flag salute (effort, behavior, kindness).
- Class of the Month: announced monthly at flag salute; the class keeps the Golden Cone trophy for the month.

## Behavior (P.E. page)
- Steps: warning, quick time out (reflection area), then "On the Bench" form that guardians must sign and return to Mrs. Miller.

## Fitnessgram (P.E. page)
- Fitnessgram fitness tests are given in grade 5 (and 7 and 9). 5th graders practice and take the test; grades 1-4 practice some of the tests.
- 5th grade tests: Mile Run, 20 meter Pacer test, Push-ups, Curl-ups, Shoulder Stretch, Trunk Lift.
- More info: https://www.cooperinstitute.org/fitnessgram

## SEES Track Team (P.E. page)
- Open to grades 3-5; tryouts are optional, held during PE sometime after winter break.
- One girl and one boy per grade are picked for each event, plus one alternate of each per grade.
- Track meet: a one-day event in May at the San Marcos High School track, against five other district schools.
- Events: 50m, 100m, 400m, 4x100m Relay, Softball throw, Long jump.

# SMUSD Library at Home (district online library site) (belongs with library)

Sources: https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/home, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/ebooks, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/research-tutorials/research, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/research-tutorials/tutorials, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/virtual-book-clubs, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/podcasts/elementary-podcasts, https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/book-trailers/student-created-book-trailers (all undated; written during distance learning, some links say "Begins April 1" with no year), read 2026-09-21.

## What it is (page undated)
- SMUSD Library at Home is a district website with ebooks and audiobooks, read-aloud videos, book talks, book trailers, kids' podcasts and research tools: https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/home
- The home page lists San Elijo's library contact as Mrs. Ellis (matches the current librarian, Jennifer Ellis; see library.md).

## Free ebooks and audiobooks (ebooks page, undated)
- Libby app: create a Libby account and get a digital San Diego County Library card on your phone.
- A San Diego County Library card also gives access to Bookflix (book videos and read-alouds for elementary students).
- Carlsbad City Library's Library@Home: San Marcos residents are eligible for a card too.
- Audible Stories: free audiobook collection for TK-12, in six languages.
- The page asks kids to check with an adult before downloading apps.

## Research databases for kids (research page, undated)
- ProQuest & Britannica School, Digital Content Portal and CalSNAP are linked from https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/research-tutorials/research ; the page gives the shared username & password "sandiego".
- Video tutorials (tutorials page): how to check out an ebook from Destiny Discover, how to use Lightbox and AV2 World Language ebooks.

## Podcasts and read-alouds for kids (undated)
- Elementary podcasts the district recommends: Earsnacks, Circle Round (ages 4 to 10), Pants on Fire (ages 6-10), What If World, Wow in the World, Brains On, Kids Ask Authors.
- Read-aloud videos by SMUSD teachers and librarians and by authors, including Spanish read-alouds: https://sites.google.com/smusd.org/library-at-home/read-alouds

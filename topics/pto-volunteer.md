## Volunteer clearance: which level (district)
Source: https://www.smusd.org/144243_3 (same page as https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/200687_2; "2026-2027 Volunteer Applications Are Now Open"), read 2026-09-21.
- Every volunteer needs district clearance, day, night or weekend, including band, PTO and athletic events and extracurricular activities.
- Level A: 5 or more hours per week. Required for anyone who goes on a field trip (effective December 1, 2025).
- Level A needs: Megan's Law clearance, TB clearance, Live Scan fingerprinting (SMUSD pays), online Mandated Reporter training, and reading the Volunteer Handbook. Level A volunteers get an SMUSD Volunteer Badge.
- Level B: less than 5 hours per week. Needs Megan's Law clearance, TB clearance, online Mandated Reporter training, and reading the Volunteer Handbook (no Live Scan).
- Not sure how often you'll help? The district suggests applying for Level A for flexibility.
- Level A application (new and returning): https://app.droplet.io/form/KJRQMK
- Level B application (new and returning): https://app.droplet.io/form/ybxeDK
- Applications and information are available in English and Spanish.

## Volunteer clearance: timing, renewal, cost
Source: https://www.smusd.org/144243_3, read 2026-09-21.
- Allow 10-12 business days for a volunteer application to be processed (2026-27 SEES principal newsletters; the district
  page says 7-10, so plan on the longer) (conflict rule: newer official source wins; stricter answer when it affects attendance or safety).
- Applications are processed in the order received. Don't call or email HR for status; an automated email arrives when clearance is final.
- Clearance does not roll over: every volunteer submits a new application every school year.
- The Mandated Reporter certificate must be from the current school year; certificates can't be sent by email.
- TB: the page asks everyone to resubmit the TB Risk Assessment Form because last year's file can't be accessed (its FAQ also says the TB risk assessment is due every 4 years).
- Live Scan is done once. A returning Level A volunteer with continuous yearly enrollment doesn't redo it. A Live Scan done for another district or league doesn't count; new Level A volunteers do one coded for SMUSD (instructions come by email after the application is processed).
- Returning Level A volunteers keep their badge and get a 2026 – 2027 sticker from the school's office manager.
- Cost: the only cost to the volunteer is a TB test or TB risk assessment. SMUSD pays for fingerprinting.
- A government-issued ID (from any country) is required.
- Level A questions: Marion Bowen, HR, (760) 752-1249, marion.bowen@smusd.org; Rose Meraz, (760) 290-2647, rose.meraz@smusd.org.
- Level B questions: contact the school where you want to volunteer (SEES office, see facts.md).

## Rules while volunteering
Source: SMUSD Volunteer Handbook https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eAhRr94bWxVAaZnoH20aZplfzE1d-NY_UlApUfiuGdY/edit (linked from the 2026-27 volunteer page; undated), read 2026-09-21.
- Sign in and out every time on the Ident-A-Kid system in the school's front office, and wear your volunteer badge.
- No bringing your own children (including siblings) while volunteering; no exceptions.
- Don't photograph or video record students.
- Never be alone with a student, never transport students, and don't give students medication or medical treatment.
- Student information is confidential; volunteers have no access to student records.
- Use adult restrooms only; phones on silent in class.
- If you can't make a volunteer shift, tell the teacher or site administrator as early as possible.
Source: Room Parent Packet 2026/2027 https://docs.google.com/document/d/10fVgGY3tMrWU5ChobG-KcBybnj_8i9NqtLjq3I8X0pE/edit, read 2026-09-21.
- SEES volunteers sign in at the front office with a valid photo ID and don't discipline students (tell the teacher).

## Room parents and class parties
Sources: https://seespto.org/pages/room-parents (2026/27 packet), Room Parent Packet 2026/2027 (link above), Party Donation Request Form https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ilH53kvgzgkP6UQU832-1DQa9pm2pYLNZylMKbzxDCA/edit, Classroom Directory Permission 2026-2027 https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gbAAj5yiAXRYdrJKyq9eF4rfWOaHVjLaHwLRHpjUQOk/edit, Party Planning Teacher Interview https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Hp5fLq5uAQvIPgl25IpDpNIP9XzVvXsc/view. Read 2026-09-21.
- Room Parent Coordinator: roomparents@seespto.org.
- Up to three class parties a year (teacher interview form: 90 minutes max each), plus an end-of-year grade-level party.
- Party donation: a suggested $10 per child, completely voluntary, for all three parties' supplies; cash in an envelope labeled "Room Parent" or Venmo. No child is left out for not paying.
- Room parents don't ask for any other money that year. Teacher birthday and Teacher Appreciation gifts are optional, with no money collected.
- Classroom directory: families opt in on the Directory Permission form (email contact, directory listing, and an option to tell the room parent about a child's allergy). Return it to class.
- Siblings can't attend class parties (district policy); parent helpers bring photo ID and sign in at the office.
- Parties are planned with the teacher and sent to PTO on the Party Approval Form at least two weeks before:
  https://forms.gle/ATkpxks7vYZ6vHNR6 (linked on seespto.org/pages/room-parents, 2026/27 page, read 2026-09-18).
- Class photos go on a private Google Photos album, not social media.

## Everyday ways to raise money
- Box Tops (https://seespto.org/pages/box-tops-for-education, undated; https://www.boxtops4education.com/s/get-the-app, read 2026-09-21): download the Box Tops app, buy Box Tops products, scan your receipt within 14 days of purchase. Linking Box Tops and Walmart accounts earns without scanning.
- Ralphs (https://seespto.org/pages/ralph-s-community-contribution-program, undated): create a Ralphs digital account, link your Shopper's Card, then choose the organization by name or NPO number at https://www.ralphs.com/account/communityrewards. SEES's NPO number isn't posted.
- Dine-outs (https://seespto.org/pages/dine-out, last updated 2024-25): the PTO announces them in the weekly newsletter, ParentSquare alerts and backpack flyers the week before. No 2026-27 dine-outs are posted.

## Book fair how-to
Sources: https://seespto.org/pages/book-fair (STALE, 2023-24; how-to only) and https://bookfairs.scholastic.com/content/fairs/how-ewallet-works.html?fairId=5694313, read 2026-09-21. Fair dates: school-dates.md.

- Scholastic eWallet instead of cash: create one per student for each fair at the Scholastic site; share the link so grandparents can add money. Unspent eWallet money becomes a Scholastic eGift Card balance for the next fair.
- You can buy a book from the teacher wish bins to donate to the classroom library.

## Speech-language services (SEES Speech Services page, page undated)
- Speech-language pathologists assess, monitor progress and treat: speech sounds, resonance, voice, fluency (stuttering), understanding and using language, social (pragmatic) language, cognition, feeding and swallowing.
- Signs the page lists: hard to understand (substituting or leaving out sounds), mixing up verb tenses, trouble telling stories, repeating or stretching sounds, a harsh/breathy/nasal voice, trouble following multi-step directions, trouble with eye contact or taking turns in conversation.
- The SEES speech-language pathologist is not named on the site.

## School psychologist (SEES School Psychologist page, page undated)
- Works with teachers, parents and administrators on learning and behavior problems.
- Does special education eligibility evaluations.
- Gives short-term counseling to special education students when their IEP calls for it.
- Helps the school handle crises (death, illness, community trauma).
- The SEES school psychologist is not named on the site.

## Special education busing (district page, page undated)
- Busing is decided in the IEP: if the IEP team lists transportation as a related service, Special Ed tells Transportation.
- A new transportation application is needed every school year: https://app.droplet.io/form/NARLRK
- Once approved, you get an email with the stop, route and pick-up/drop-off times.
- One permanent morning stop and one permanent afternoon stop only; changes must be approved by Special Education.
- Be at the stop 5 minutes before pick-up; the bus won't honk or wait.
- Absent: tell the driver a day ahead, or call Transportation between 5:00 am and 5:00 pm at 760.290.2654.
- No ride 2 days in a row without telling Transportation: the bus stops coming until you call to resume.
- A parent or the designated adult on the application must meet the bus; otherwise the child goes to a receiving location or the police.
- Transportation Department (routes, schedules): 760-290-2670, bus.info@smusd.org
- Dispatch after hours: 760.290.2670
- AM Dispatcher Flossie Lopez: 760.290.2655, flossie.lopez@smusd.org, office hours 4:30 AM – 1:00 PM
- PM Dispatcher Dawn Bush: 760.290.2654, dawn.bush@smusd.org, office hours 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM
- Bus aides and wheelchairs/medical equipment: an aide is provided if the IEP says so; all buses carry mobility devices that can be secured safely.

## GATE (gifted): who gets tested and when (SEES GATE page, page undated)
- GATE testing is in 3rd grade. Applications are handed out at parent-teacher conferences.
- 3rd and 4th graders can be referred by a parent or the classroom teacher at the start of the school year.
- Test: Otis-Lennon School Ability Test (OLSAT). Testing is in Feb/March ("usually in the spring").
- Eligibility uses the OLSAT, state test scores, and teacher and parent questionnaires.
- Parents are told in April/May whether their child qualified.
- Not yet in 3rd grade: wait until 3rd grade for the application.
- 4th grade: can be referred at the start of the year (bring it up at conferences); if they qualify, GATE starts in 5th grade.
- 5th grade: cannot be tested.
- Didn't qualify in 3rd grade but came close: can be retested in 4th grade with a parent or teacher recommendation.
- Results follow district protocol; the page says there's no reconsideration outside retesting.
- GATE is elementary only in SMUSD; middle/high school Honors and AP are open to all students.

## GATE: what it looks like (SEES GATE page, page undated)
- No separate GATE class and no pull-outs: GATE students are clustered in a regular classroom with a teacher trained for gifted students.
- Services start the school year after a child qualifies. In the fall, students get an interest survey and parents a volunteer interest form.
- Optional after-school GATE enrichment: typically 1-2 activities a month, all ending no later than 4:15.
- Programs can change year to year and differ between schools, depending on each site's GATE budget.
- Child bored in class: the page says they must first show mastery of required work; then encourage them to propose their own project to the teacher.
- The page names Mr. Pope as GATE manager; he is the former principal. Who runs GATE at SEES in 2026-27 is not published.

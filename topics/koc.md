# Kids on Campus (KOC): before- and after-school care

Sources: https://www.smusd.org/koc-information-and-faqs (school year 2026-27), https://www.smusd.org/kids-on-campus-extended-day-home (undated), https://www.smusd.org/100879_3 (undated), https://sanmarcosusd8.smartsiteshost.com/kids-on-campus-koc (undated). All read 2026-09-18.
KOC is run by the district (SMUSD), not by SEES. San Elijo Elementary is one of its 9 sites (grades TK-5).

## Hours (KOC FAQ page)
- KOC school-year hours: 6:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.
- KOC is closed on all school holidays, plus one day per semester for staff professional development (dates stated in the contract).
- KOC Camp hours (school breaks): 7:00 am to 5:00 pm.
- KOC dates, closures and minimum days: 2026-27 Parent Calendar: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_parent_calendar_-_2026-2027__4-10-26_1.pdf (Camp days are shown in yellow).

## Shifts (KOC FAQ page)
- AM shift: 6:30-8:45, before-school care for TK-5th graders.
- Before TK Care: 8:15-11:51, for students in PM TK; they eat lunch at KOC and staff walk them to class at 11:51.
- After TK Care: 11:51-3:10, for students in AM TK; staff pick them up from class at 11:51 and they eat lunch at KOC.
- After TK Care on Early Release Wednesdays: staff pick students up at 10:51; they may stay at KOC until 3:10.
- PM shift: 3:10-6:00, after-school care for TK-5th graders.
- PM shift on Early Release Wednesdays: KOC receives students 1 hour early, at 2:10; open until 6:00 pm as usual.
- 5 days of care are required for any shift. No part-time schedules.
- Shift details by site: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/shifts_of_koc_care_offered_by_site__2025-26.pdf (document is labelled 2025-26).

## How to sign up (KOC FAQ page)
- Register online only, in the EZ ChildTrack Parent Portal: http://www.ezchildtrack.com/kidsoncampus/parent. No paper or in-person registration.
- 2026-27 registration dates and details: KOC 2026-27 Enrollment page https://www.smusd.org/201705_3 (not read by us).
- Enrollment is first come, first served. Past families are not guaranteed a spot. When a site or grade room is full, there is a waitlist.
- EZ ChildTrack emails you when the application is submitted (pending), when your child is enrolled, or if your child is waitlisted.
- 2026-27 contract: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/2026-27_kids_on_campus_program_contract_no_initials_space_ez_childtrack_websitedocx.pdf
- 2026-27 statement of consent: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/statement_of_consent_2026-27.pdf
- 2026-27 parent handbook: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_parent_handbook_2026-27.pdf (read 2026-09-21 for tuition rules)

- KOC Half-Day TK (8:15-11:51 or 11:51-3:10), TK: $154.00 a week; $308.00 (2-week month), $462.00 (3-week month), $616.00 (4-week month).
- KOC AM + Half-Day TK, TK: $239.00 a week; $478.00 (2-week month), $717.00 (3-week month), $956.00 (4-week month).
- KOC Half-Day TK + PM, TK: $282.00 a week; $564.00 (2-week month), $846.00 (3-week month), $1,128.00 (4-week month).

- KOC commitment: at least 4 weeks of tuition; 2 weeks' notice to change schedules or leave (2026-27 parent handbook).
- KOC fees are based on enrollment, not attendance (2026-27 parent handbook).
- KOC Camp price (2026-27): $50.00 per full day plus any field trip costs (2026-27 rates sheet); the Summer Camp 2026 FAQ
  says $250 for a 5-day week. Last year's $240 a week is out of date; don't quote it (conflict rule: newer official source wins; stricter answer when it affects attendance or safety).

## Cost and payment (KOC FAQ page; payment page 100879_3)
- KOC registration fee: $50, due at enrollment, by electronic check or credit card (all cards except American Express).
- KOC Camp cost: see the 2026-27 price above (shorter holiday weeks are prorated).
- Credit/debit card convenience fee: 3% on all card payments. No fee for cash or check.
- FSA debit cards: not accepted by KOC.
- Financial help: KOC does not offer scholarships, but accepts subsidy payments from CDA (Child Development Associates, 619-427-4411, https://cdasd.org/families/childcare/resource-center/) and YMCA Childcare Resource Service (https://www.ymcasd.org/family-resources/paying-child-care).
- Military families may also receive assistance from Child Care Aware.

## Drop-off and pick-up (KOC FAQ page)
- Children must be signed in and out by a parent, a sibling in 3rd grade or above (Release to Sibling form: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/release_to_older_sibling__p-u_and_d-o_3rd_and_up_1.pdf), or someone on the emergency sheet. Be ready to show ID.
- Sign-in/out uses EZ ChildTrack PINs. Parents create PINs for authorized pick-up people in the Parent Portal.
- KOC does not take children to or from the bus, and bus drivers do not walk children to KOC.

## Absences (KOC FAQ page)
- If your child will miss PM KOC for any reason, call your child's KOC room during the day. Give your full name, child's full name, grade and the date.
- If you don't call, KOC staff will call you to check your child is accounted for.

## Daily life (KOC FAQ page)
- KOC PM snack: served around 3:40 each afternoon; monthly menu on the parent board. Kids may also eat a snack from home.
- Allergies: staff see the allergies on the emergency sheet; tell a staff member about specific instructions.
- Homework: grades 1-5 have homework time with a leader supervising; no one-on-one tutoring. Ask to add your child to the Homework Club List.
- Activities: a leader-led activity and a supervised group game each shift (art, STEM, Makerspace, Chromebooks, SEL), plus free play.
- No nap time (there is a quiet time). All children, including TKs, must be fully potty trained.
- Communication: phone calls about your child's day; parent board above the sign-in area; EZ ChildTrack emails. Every family should expect some incident reports.

## After-school and morning activities with KOC (KOC FAQ page)
- FitKids or other 3rd-party on-site activity: fill out the Release to 3rd Party On-Site Activity form: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/kids_on_campus-release_to_3rd_party_on-site_activity_1.pdf. New form each time the schedule changes.
- School-run activity (chorus, rehearsal, intervention): Release to On-Site Activity form; say if your child returns to KOC or you pick up at the dismissal point at 4:15 (3:15 on Wednesdays).
- At San Elijo, a Fitkids Liaison walks kinders from KOC (or their classroom, for PM kinders) to their activity and back to KOC.
- Before-school activity: Release to On-Site Activities form: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/release_to_on-site_activity_1.pdf. Leaders walk your child there.
- AM KOC kids (1st-5th) can go to morning recess at 8:15 with the Release to Morning Recess form: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/release_to_am_recess_pdf_1.pdf. Class bell is 8:45.

## Camp (KOC FAQ page)
- KOC Camp: Thanksgiving, Winter, Spring and Summer; full-day, 7:00 am to 5:00 pm; $50.00 per full day (2026-27 rates sheet).
- Camp sign-up is separate, on EZ ChildTrack, opening about one month before each break. Whole weeks only: you sign up for a camp week as a whole, and a short holiday week costs only its days ($50 a day, so the 3-day Thanksgiving camp, Nov 23-25, is $150 plus any field trip).
- Camp runs at fewer sites than the school year. More: https://smusd.org/135299_3

## TK and KOC (TK schedule options 2026-27; TK FAQs; Spring 2026 TK presentation)
Sources: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_tk_schedule_options_26-27.pdf (2026-27),
https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_tk_info_sessions_and_site_tours_spring_2026.pdf (Spring 2026),
https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_tk_faqs_before_tk_care.pdf and https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_tk_faqs_after_tk_care.pdf (both undated). Read 2026-09-21.
- PM TK child: Before TK KOC Care 8:15-11:51, then PM TK school 11:51-3:27; can add AM KOC 6:30-8:15 and KOC PM 3:27-6:00.
- AM TK child: AM TK school 8:15-11:51, then After TK KOC Care 11:51-3:10; can add AM KOC 6:30-8:15 and KOC PM 3:10-6:00.
- Before TK drop-off: at or after 8:15 am; a parent must bring the child into the KOC room and sign in. Children can arrive any time between 8:15 and 11:51 am.
- At 11:51 KOC staff walk Before TK children to their TK teacher; for After TK Care, staff pick children up from the TK teacher and walk them to KOC. TKs never go anywhere unattended.
- After TK Care ends at 3:10 PM: parents come into the KOC room to sign out; pick up by 3:20 or late fees of $1 per minute apply (Spring 2026 presentation).
- Lunch: TKs in Before or After TK Care eat lunch at KOC; pack a lunch, or KOC staff walk them to get cafeteria lunch (free for all SMUSD students). Tell the KOC Leaders your lunch preference in person or by phone.
- Breakfast: only with Early AM care (6:30-8:15); AM Leaders take students to breakfast at 8:00 am. Before TK care has no breakfast time.
- TK KOC ratio is 10:1; half-day TK KOC class size is at most 20; at least 2 adults on every shift (Spring 2026 presentation).
- No nap (quiet time instead). Children must be fully potty trained; staff cannot help with wiping or pull-ups.
- A sibling in 3rd grade or above can drop off a TK child only with the release form, returned to the KOC site.
- The KOC room location is sent in an info letter before school starts. Questions: KOC office 760-752-1279.
- TKs may attend Thanksgiving, Winter and Spring Camps; Summer Camp does not take incoming TKs.
- Older undated Before TK FAQ says PM KOC is 3:25-6:00; the 2026-27 schedule says 3:27-6:00. Use 3:27.

## Rules from the 2026-27 Parent Handbook
Source: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_parent_handbook_2026-27.pdf (2026-27), read 2026-09-21.
- Late pick-up: $1.00 per minute after your shift's end time. Late more than five times in a school year and you may be dropped.
- If you'll be late, arrange for someone on the Emergency Information form to pick up before the end time. Not picked up 10 minutes after end time and no call: KOC contacts your authorized people.
- School-year PM care closes at 6:00 p.m. ($1.00 per minute after 6:00 p.m., Fee Information 2026-27). If no one can be reached within one hour of closing, KOC must contact the Sheriff's Office or Child Protective Services. Camp closes at 5:00 pm; same rule.
- Not a drop-in program: all 5 days of a weekly schedule. Each child starts with a one-month trial period.
- Only children enrolled in SMUSD elementary schools are eligible. Students must be enrolled in SMUSD before enrolling in KOC (smusd.org/news-and-announcements).
- Enrolling at a school other than your SMUSD attendance-boundary school means losing the spot (Fee Information 2026-27).
- Illness credit: illness over three consecutive days may be credited from the fourth day on, with a doctor's note.
- Vacation credit: up to two weeks during the school year, consecutive days, pre-approved by the Director (not for Camp).
- Unpaid by the 15th of the month: child dropped; $50.00 re-registration fee plus all money owed to return. Returned (NSF) checks: $15.00 fee.
- Payments can't be left at the center or given to staff. Schedule changes or leaving: 2 weeks' notice to the District Office, not center staff.
- Sign in/out: come into the building; each authorized adult has their own confidential PIN. No sign-in needed when a child comes to KOC straight from class (KOC takes roll).
- Transitions: staff walk TK and kinder to and from class. 1st-5th graders walk to KOC on their own after school; tell the teacher your child goes to KOC.
- Morning 1st-5th graders can go to the playground at 8:15 (with the form) or stay in the KOC room until the bell.
- After-school classes on campus need prior written permission; children check in at KOC before going to the class.
- Sick: children too sick for school can't attend KOC; temperature of 100.5 or above means you'll be called to pick up.
- Medication only with the Authorization For Medication Administration form plus doctor's orders, in the original labelled container. Children may not carry medication.
- Food: KOC gives an afternoon snack; no candy, gum or soda; KOC does not knowingly serve peanut butter or nut products.
- Split custody: parents must agree on one Emergency Information form; split billing needs the Contract for Split-Pay Accounts.

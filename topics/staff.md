principal, office or a specialist usually means news for the whole school or a grade. It's not complete: the school
doesn't publish a staff directory, and staff change during the year. Only published email addresses are listed;
never guess one. For anyone not here, the parent can look in ParentVUE (teacher contacts) or call the office.

## KOC Camp: how it works (KOC Camps page, undated)
Sources: https://smusd.org/135299_3 (fetched 2026-09-21); https://www.smusd.org/news-and-announcements (2026-27, read 2026-09-18).
- KOC Camp is full-day care during Thanksgiving, Winter, Spring and Summer breaks. Enrollment is by the week.
- Any SMUSD student may use KOC Camp, even if they don't attend KOC during the school year.
- KOC Camps are announced in the News and Announcements section (https://smusd.org/news-and-announcements) about 4-6 weeks before each Camp. Spaces fill up quickly.
- Camp sites change each break (district maintenance projects); sites are announced with the Camp details. Families pick one Camp location per Camp.
- TKs may attend school-year Camps (Thanksgiving, Winter & Spring) but not Summer Camp. Summer Camp is for SMUSD students entering K-5.
- Sign-up is in EZ ChildTrack, separately from school-year KOC. No child is accepted at Camp unless the Camp Registration is approved in EZ ChildTrack (Fee Information 2026-27).

## KOC Camp rules (Summer Camp 2026 FAQ; 2025-26 Camp FAQ; 2026-27 Parent Handbook)
Sources: Summer Camp 2026 FAQ https://docs.google.com/document/d/1U1vyLf9HbWf1Sk47oevX8OkM9vKSw1Sw3L-If83UzJc/edit?tab=t.0;
KOC Camp 2025-26 FAQ https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y-kKXkKG5QReioln_CzJ3pJSJrJKElwihycGgu-chZE/edit?tab=t.0 (linked as the "most recent" Camp FAQ);
Parent Handbook https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/koc_parent_handbook_2026-27.pdf. All read 2026-09-21. The FAQs are updated for each Camp.
- KOC Camp hours for 2026-27: 7:00 am to 5:00 pm (half an hour later start and 1 hour earlier close than school-year KOC). Late pick-up fees apply after 5:00 pm.
- Drop off and pick up any time; most Camp activities are between 8:00 am and 4:30 pm. Field-trip days have a drop-off cutoff time, given in the Camp Info Letter.
- KOC Camp cost (Summer Camp 2026 FAQ): $50 per day, so a 5-day week is $250; shorter weeks prorated; field-trip weeks cost extra (venue + bus).
- KOC Camp price 2026-27: $50.00 per full day (2026-27 rates sheet); $250 for a 5-day week (Summer Camp 2026 FAQ). Last year's
  $48 a day is out of date (conflict rule: newer official source wins; stricter answer when it affects attendance or safety).
- No refunds for Camp: you pay for the weeks you sign up for, used or not. You can add weeks if there's room, but can't swap or drop booked weeks.
- Skipping days in a booked Camp week is fine, but the weekly fee stays the same.
- Illness: no refunds, except the handbook rule (illness over three consecutive days, credit from the fourth day with a doctor's note).
- Food: KOC gives an AM and PM snack. Send a packed lunch every day; cafeterias are closed during Camp.
- Bring: water bottle, jacket, sunscreen (summer) and packed lunch in a backpack; a change of clothes for kindergarteners. No toys from home.
- Summer sunscreen: in EZ ChildTrack registration you choose whether staff reapply sunscreen.
- Field trips: included in that week's price and can't be removed; you may keep your child home that day. No stay-back care at the center on trip days.
- Field trips: be at the center by the stated time or your child can't get on the bus; children can't be picked up at the trip site and must ride the bus back.
- Red KOC Camp field-trip t-shirt: $10, strongly recommended for trip weeks; one shirt works for future Camps.
- Camp ratio: 14:1 (10:1 for TK- and kinder-only classes); lower on trips. Parents can't chaperone trips.
- Camp eligibility may depend on a review of the child's KOC disciplinary history (handbook).

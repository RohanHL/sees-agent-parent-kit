# Topic briefs

Curated, sourced briefs on the things SEES parents ask about. Don't load
these upfront — fetch the one that matches the parent's question, via its
raw URL:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RohanHL/sees-agent-parent-kit/main/topics/<file>`

- `camps-afterschool.md` — camps and after-school options (KOC Camp dates, ASES/Expanded Learning)
- `enrollment-attendance.md` — enrollment, residency verification, attendance rules
- `food.md` — meal accounts, applications, party/fundraiser food rules
- `general-academics.md` — academics overview
- `general-communication.md` — how the school communicates (ParentSquare, etc.)
- `general-district-calendar.md` — district calendar notes
- `general-family-support.md` — family support resources
- `health.md` — health requirements, immunizations, medications at school
- `koc.md` — KOC (Kids on Campus) childcare: daily care, prices, closures
- `library.md` — school library
- `mental-health-wellness.md` — mental health and wellness resources
- `pe.md` — PE
- `pto-volunteer.md` — PTO, volunteering, clearance levels
- `safety-policies.md` — safety policies and procedures
- `student-support.md` — student support services
- `student-support-english-learners.md` — English learner support
- `technology.md` — Chromebooks, accounts, tech policies
- `transportation.md` — buses, pickup/dropoff
- `web-community-resources.md` — community resources on the web
- `web-library-at-home.md` — library resources at home

Each brief carries its sources. Answer from the brief, cite per claim —
same rules as `school-facts.md`.

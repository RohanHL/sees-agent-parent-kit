# Food at school: meal accounts, applications, party and fundraiser food rules

Meals are free for every student (see facts.md "Lunch and breakfast"); menus: `school/prep.py` / facts.md.
This file covers everything else parents ask about food.

## Meal account (SchoolCafe)
Source: https://www.smusd.org/online-meal-applications (page undated), read 2026-09-21.
- SMUSD uses SchoolCafe for student meal accounts: https://www.schoolcafe.com/SANMARCOSUSD
- In SchoolCafe a parent can: make payments, set purchase restrictions, submit meal applications, view purchases, set up auto pay, get low balance alerts, and view letters.
- Refund of money left in a meal account: fill in the online Student Meal Account Refund Request (SchoolCafe refund form): https://app.droplet.io/form/Nwa4L0 (link from the CNS home page below).

## Meal application (optional) and food help
Source: https://www.smusd.org/child-nutrition-services-home (page undated), read 2026-09-21.
- No meal application is needed for the free breakfast and lunch.
- The confidential meal application is still worth filling in (on SchoolCafe): depending on eligibility it can connect a family to SUN Bucks summer food benefits, reduced fees for certain school activities, tests or services, and other local, state or community assistance.
- It can also help schools qualify for extra funding (California's Local Control Funding Formula). Participation is optional and private.
- Help with the application: contact Child Nutrition Services (contacts below).
- CalFresh (food stamps / SNAP / EBT): apply at https://www.getcalfresh.org/ ("in as little as ten minutes").

## Class parties: pizza from the cafeteria
Source: Room Parent Packet 2026/2027 https://docs.google.com/document/d/10fVgGY3tMrWU5ChobG-KcBybnj_8i9NqtLjq3I8X0pE/edit (school year 2026-27), read 2026-09-21.
- Food at class parties is optional and should not be the focus; any food served must follow the district Smart Snacks guidelines.
- Pizza from the cafeteria can be served at class parties for no fee: it counts as each child's free lunch that day, so the child can't get a second meal.
- A child who needs an alternative to pizza: it can be arranged when the order is placed (the form has an "Alternate Entree" and a "Requesting Special Diet meal" box).
- Pizza luncheon order must be submitted at least 1 week in advance, to the cafeteria.
- Classroom luncheon prices on the order form: students $0.00; adults $5.00; additional pizza (1 only) $10.00; delivery fee $20.00.
- Included: 16" pre-sliced pizza (pepperoni or cheese) or alternate entree, Carroteenies, salad with Italian dressing, fresh fruit, Raisels, ranch dressing.
- Pickup from the kitchen at the time on the form, or classroom delivery for a $20 charge (approved when ordering). Delivery time must be 15 to 30 minutes before or after lunch serve times.
- The teacher pays any amount due in cash or check when the food arrives, and marks a roster; meals not on the roster are charged at the adult price.
Source: Party Planning Teacher Interview https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Hp5fLq5uAQvIPgl25IpDpNIP9XzVvXsc/view (undated), read 2026-09-21.
- Room parents ask the teacher which parties get food, whether to serve cafeteria pizza, whether to hold non-food parties, and which foods to avoid for allergies.

## Food sold at school (fundraisers, bake sales to students)
Source: https://www.smusd.org/smart-snacksfundraisers (page undated), read 2026-09-21.
- To sell food or drinks at school you need a Food and Beverage Request Form: request one by calling 760-752-1286.
- Questions about Smart Snacks or food/beverage sales: Terri Mitchell, Child Nutrition Services, terri.mitchell@smusd.org, (760) 752-1286.
- District Pre-Approved Snack List for fundraisers: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/pre-approved_snack_list.pdf
- Smart Snacks Guide: https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/smart_snacks_-_complete_guide.pdf
Source: California Dept. of Education quick reference card https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/4153/quick_reference_smart_snack.pdf (effective 1/1/2017; linked from the district page above as current), read 2026-09-21.
- The rules cover ALL food and drinks SOLD to students by anyone (PTO, clubs, anyone), from midnight until half an hour after the school day ends.
- A compliant elementary snack must be a fruit, vegetable, dairy, protein or whole grain item (or have one first in the ingredients), or contain at least 1/4 cup of fruit or vegetable.
- Per item: 200 calories or less, 200 milligrams sodium or less, under 0.5 grams trans fat (no exceptions).
- Also: no more than 35% of calories from fat, under 10% of calories from saturated fat, no more than 35% sugar by weight (nuts, seeds, fruit and some cheeses are exempt from some of these).
- Drinks allowed in elementary: water (no added sweeteners); milk (1% unflavored or nonfat); non-dairy milk equal to milk; fruit/vegetable juice that is at least 50% juice, no added sweeteners, 8 fl. oz. or less. All drinks caffeine-free.
- Student organization sales: one item per sale, pre-approved by the district board, only after the last lunch period, not prepared on campus, and at most four sales per school per year.
- Sellers must keep their own records proving compliance. The district's wellness policy can be stricter.

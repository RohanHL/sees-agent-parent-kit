## ELPAC (English language test for English learners)
Source: ELPAC Testing Presentation, https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1uMnkMLnQ5QuasKmyIrcfJqWd0zIOfiHFZhPp3aLKyjE/edit (linked from the SEES Resources page; undated), read 2026-09-21.
- The ELPAC is California's English language proficiency test for students whose primary language is not English. Emergent bilingual students take it every year to measure progress.
- Kindergarten and Grade 1 take it one-on-one (1:1).
- Every student who takes it gets a score report: results in each of the 4 domains and an overall score on a 1 to 4 scale.
- Questions? Start with your child's teacher.
- A practice test lets students and families see the types of questions.

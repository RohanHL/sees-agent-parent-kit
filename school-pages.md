# School map: San Elijo Elementary (SEES)

Where to look for anything the school facts, dates, menu and topic briefs don't cover. Three parts: which school
system does what, school-wide documents and forms (83), and every page on the school, district and PTO websites
(231, checked live 2026-10-29). Find the likely place here, open it, and answer from what it says now; pages
and documents change. Parent-made, not an official school or district index. Nothing here is about one class or
family: the parent's own school mail covers that.

## Where things live: the school's systems

Which system does what, so you can point the parent to the right place. Details, steps and sources are in the topic
brief named on each line (topic briefs index, listed in the your-school guide). These are the parent's own accounts:
tell them where to go; never sign in for them.

- **Who's who**: the office, principal, every classroom teacher by grade, and the specialists (PE, library, STEM,
  music), for telling who a school message is from. Brief: `staff.md`.
- **ParentSquare**: school messages by email, text and app, including the principal's newsletter (usually weekly);
  messaging teachers and staff; signing forms and permission slips; volunteer sign-ups. Accounts are created from
  the contact details given at registration. Emails come from donotreply@parentsquare.com. Brief:
  `general-communication.md`.
- **ParentVUE** (Synergy): enrolling, grades, assignments, attendance, teacher contacts, and the family's phone and
  email on file (ParentSquare picks up changes the next day). One account covers all of a family's SMUSD students.
  Brief: `enrollment-attendance.md`.
- **Peachjar**: school and community flyers, posted and emailed Thursday evenings, usually as images. Brief:
  `general-communication.md`.
- **Droplet** (app.droplet.io): district online forms, such as bus pass applications, volunteer clearance (Level A
  and B) and meal account refunds. Bus confirmations come from no-reply@mail.droplet.io. Briefs:
  `transportation.md`, `pto-volunteer.md`.
- **EZ ChildTrack**: Kids on Campus (before- and after-school care) registration and parent portal. Brief: `koc.md`.
- **Here Comes the Bus**: live bus tracking app. Brief: `transportation.md`.
- **SchoolCafe**: student meal accounts and meal applications. Brief: `food.md`.
- **YumYummi**: breakfast and lunch menus with nutrition and allergens. This week's menu is in the school pack's menu
  file.
- **Smore**: web newsletters some staff send (for example PE and the library), usually linked from a ParentSquare
  post. Briefs: `pe.md`, `library.md`.
- **seespto.org**: the PTO's events, volunteering, room parents and spirit wear. Brief: `pto-volunteer.md`.

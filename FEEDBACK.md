# Help improve this kit

This kit gets better when parents speak up. You don't need a GitHub account,
you don't need to be technical — your assistant does the work.

## Two kinds of feedback

**1. "Something's wrong"** — a date looks off, a sender is missing, a fact
is outdated.
→ Just tell your assistant: *"the Fun Run is actually Nov 21, not Nov 19."*
It will check your email for the source, draft a correction note, and ask if
you want it sent to Rohan. One tap from you; he verifies and updates the
shared files for everyone.

**2. "I wish it worked differently"** — the digest is too long, setup
confused you, you want it to watch something new.
→ Tell your assistant in plain words. It will package your feedback —
what happened, what you expected — and offer to pass it along. This is how
the setup flow and the weekly triage get better: real usage, real notes.

## What happens next

Rohan reads every note, verifies corrections against the source, and updates
the kit. When your feedback ships, it lands in `CHANGELOG.md` below with
your first name — so you (and other parents) can see the kit improving.

## The ground rules

- Corrections need a source (a newsletter, the school site, the office).
  "I think picture day moved" isn't enough — your assistant will help find
  the proof.
- Family-specific stuff never leaves your chat. Only shared school
  information goes into the kit.
- Nothing here changes without Rohan verifying it first. Your note is a
  signal, not an edit.

# Questions, corrections

This kit is maintained lightly and mostly by automation. If you spot
something wrong or want to reach Rohan, here's how.

## "Something's wrong with a date or fact"

Tell your assistant — *"the Fun Run is actually Nov 21, not Nov 19"* — and
ask it to draft a correction note. It will pull the source from your email
and write it up; you forward it to Rohan. He verifies against the source
and the shared files get updated for everyone.

Corrections need a source (a newsletter, the school site, the office).
"I think picture day moved" isn't enough — your assistant can help find
the proof.

## "I want to reach Rohan"

Your assistant can draft the message; you send it. That's the whole loop —
no accounts, no follow-ups.

## Ground rules

- Family-specific stuff never leaves your chat. Only shared school
  information goes into a note.
- Nothing changes without verification first. Your note is a signal, not
  an edit.

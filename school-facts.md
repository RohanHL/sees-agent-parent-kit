## Enrollment and residency verification (smusd.org/33593_4, verified live 2026-09-19)
- **Current students only:** SMUSD requires every current family to complete residency verification each year so
  continuing students are properly registered. It began June 2026 for 2026-27 and is **annual**.
- It is done **digitally in ParentVUE** (log in → Online Registration → Residency Verification). One ParentVUE
  account covers every student in the household, so a family submits once for all kids at the same address.
- Two proofs of residency, **one from each category, both current**:
  - Category One: mortgage statement or payment receipt, rental contract/lease or payment receipts, property tax
    receipt, Grant Deed, escrow papers.
  - Category Two: utility service contract/statement/receipt, pay stub, voter registration, correspondence from a
    government agency, or a signed Affidavit of Residence.
- No scanner? Phone scan apps are listed on the page (Adobe Scan, Genius Scan, CamScanner, etc.). The page has
  English and Spanish video walkthroughs.
- Help: each school has a ParentVUE staff member; district ParentVUE help desk parentvuehelp@smusd.org. New
  students enroll through the district enrollment page instead (smusd.org/101100_3, 2026-27).

  with a parent for a tardy slip.

## Health office (SEES Health Office page, undated, read 2026-09-19)
- Health Aide: **Kelly Godfrey**, **(760) 290-2608**, kelly.godfrey@smusd.org.
- Tell the school at enrollment if a child has a health condition that may need help on campus.
- **No medication in a backpack** unless a physician has provided written documentation. Over-the-counter meds can be given only by the Health Aide, or by a parent who comes to the health office.
- Prescriptions: physician form + parent authorization, medication kept in the health office. Forms: smusd.org/medication-policy-and-forms-poliza. Allergy/anaphylaxis plan: smusd.org/allergy-information-and-forms-la-in.
- Staff do not diagnose. If a child is ill or injured, they call the ParentVUE number; pick up as soon as you can. Keep that number current (front office).
- Optional student accident insurance packets go home at the start of the year.

## Emergency dismissal (SEES Emergency Dismissal Plan + FAQ, undated, read 2026-09-19)
- A real emergency: **911**, or the office **(760) 290-2600**. That still comes first.
- Students are released **only** to people on the emergency card, **with photo ID**. Keep the card current; tell everyone on it how pickup works.
- Check in at the Family Reunion area: sign the release log, ID check, wait there until the child is brought out. Park a few blocks away so emergency vehicles can get through.
- If the campus is evacuated, students go to **San Elijo Middle School** or **San Elijo Park** (Schoolhouse Way). The school will say which. Expect to wait.
- Notification: emergency phone calls and email. If you are already on campus, follow staff.

## Absences, vacations and early pickup (SEES Attendance page)
- **Report an absence** (all day, partial or late) **by 9:00am**: email sees.attendance@smusd.org or call
  (760) 290-2600 ext 4 (24-hour line). Include the student's name, your name and relationship, the teacher, and
  the reason with symptoms. Notify the office **each day** unless there's a doctor's note; a doctor's note is
  required for absences of 4+ days.
- Excused: illness, doctor's appointments, funerals, religious observance. **Family vacations and personal days
  are not excused.**
- **Short-term independent study** covers **1–15 days** away (e.g. a family trip): request it at the front office
  **at least 5 business days before** the absence so teachers can assign work.
- Leaving early for an appointment: tell the teacher at the start of the day (email or ParentSquare).

## Chromebooks and online safety
- **Chromebooks are "Stay-at-School" this year.** Devices stay at school overnight except teacher-assigned
  special projects. TK has no student devices; K has 8 shared classroom devices; grades 1–5 have 1:1 devices
  during the school day. A **home-use opt-in form for grades 2–5 only** is for families without a computer or
  tablet at home; families with their own device need take no action.
- **Linewize** is SMUSD's web filter on district devices; it keeps filtering outside the school day, with
  settings that may differ from instructional hours. Families get complimentary **Qustodio Parent App** access
  (one license per child, view activity, manage screen time, blocked-content alerts) — Qustodio emails a
  sign-up link to the family's SMUSD parent-account address. Info: smusd.onlinesafetyhub.io. Questions go to
  the school office or the district.
- **Chromebook insurance is optional but recommended, and open for purchase through Sat Oct 31, 2026.**
  Info and claims: sites.google.com/smusd.org/smusd11parentstudentinformatio/insurance. (2026-27 principal
  newsletter, Aug 16 2026; also the Sep 4 library newsletter.)
- Handbook reminders (from the Aug 23 2026 principal newsletter): **no open-toed shoes or slides**; **smart
  watches must stay in the backpack** during the school day.

## Bus (smusd.org/generaledtransportation)
- For eligible general-education students (based on grade and distance from school).
- 2026-27 fees: $725 full-year round trip, $400 one way, $100 for a 40-ride coupon book. Apply through Droplet.
- The application itself (send this link when a parent asks for it): General Education Transportation Application,
  https://app.droplet.io/form/0kjvRK (linked from smusd.org/generaledtransportation, read 2026-09-18; opens 2026-10-01).
- **Apply and be approved before the student may ride.** Applications are processed in order, via the General
  Education Transportation Request Form on the SMUSD Transportation website. Approval arrives by email from
  no-reply@mail.droplet.io with the assigned stop and morning departure time (tell families to check spam).
  A new application may not clear during the first week of school, so have an alternate plan until it does.
- Riders use their **assigned stop and route** both ways, must be on the driver's approved rider list, and get a
  **bus pass** during the first week of school (carry it and show/scan it to board).
- Be at the stop **at least 5 minutes early**; drivers do not leave a morning stop before the scheduled time.
  Afternoon times can run late with dismissal, loading and traffic.
- **Stop or route changes** go through the same request form and must be approved (drivers cannot take a verbal request).
- Live bus tracking: the **Here Comes the Bus** app, district code **87653** (plus the student's ID number and last name).
- Kindergarten and TK riders must be met at the stop by an adult.
- Questions: bus.info@smusd.org or (760) 290-2670.

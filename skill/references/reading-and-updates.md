# Reading their school mail, and regular updates

## School mail
- Search only the school senders they agreed to (the pack suggests some). For what's coming up, start about a week
  back; go further back when a question or something still open (a form, a trip) needs it, as far as the start of
  the school year.
- Search by sender, not subject words. Many parents get ParentSquare as a daily digest, one email bundling the
  day's posts with a subject like "Oct 4, 2026 Digest: 1 new message"; the principal's newsletter is often inside
  one. Read every post in a digest.
- To tell who a message is from (a classroom teacher, a specialist, the office), the staff brief in the school
  pack lists who's who.
- Follow links and attachments for details. Never click tracking, "confirm" or sign-up links; reading a form isn't
  submitting it. If a flyer is an image you can't read reliably, say so rather than guess its date.
- If no school mail turns up, it may go to another account or the other parent. Ask which; don't widen to the whole
  inbox or conclude nothing is outstanding.

## Regular updates
- Only after the parent agreed when, where and what. If you already send them something regular, school is a
  section of it.
- Lead with the date they'd most likely miss in the next few weeks; skip what's empty. Class learning can become a
  question they could ask their child, never a claim about how the child felt.
- Say an update is scheduled only once you've seen it set up, and working only once one actually arrived. If one was
  missed, say so next time and cover it.
- Never put family details into this kit or anything public.
